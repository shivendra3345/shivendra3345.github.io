Frequently asked

What do I need to install it?

A Microsoft 365 tenant with SharePoint Online, and someone with App Catalog admin rights to deploy the package.

Where is my ticket data stored?

In SharePoint lists in your own tenant. Nothing is sent to an outside database or to us.

How do alerts and automation work?

The helpdesk is designed to work with Power Automate. You can build flows for new-ticket emails, escalations, reminders and approvals using the list data.
